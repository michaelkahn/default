# M Coffee House

**Where:** 7440 Laguna Blvd, Suite 111, Elk Grove, CA
**Category:** Coffee & Tea café

M Coffee House in Elk Grove is a great stop for matcha lovers. The
standout on my visit was their **matcha flight** — a smart way to taste
several different styles of matcha side by side without committing to a
full drink of each.

## The Matcha Flight

The flight arrived as a set of small cups lined up on a wooden flight
board, which made it easy to sip through them and compare. Each one was
good and had its own distinct character:

- **Uber Matcha**
- **Matcha Latte**
- **Matcha [and spanner?]** — *need to confirm the exact name of this
  one; captured from voice notes*
- **Matcha Coconut Blast**

Every pour was good and unique. The flight format made it a fun,
low-commitment way to explore the range of matcha they offer — a nice
option if you can't decide on a single drink or just want to taste the
differences.

## Notes

- The wooden flight board / small-cup presentation is a nice touch.
- Solid pick for anyone wanting to branch out beyond a standard matcha
  latte.
