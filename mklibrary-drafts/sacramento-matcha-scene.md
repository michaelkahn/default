# The Sacramento Matcha Scene

Matcha has gone from a niche order to a full-blown movement around
Sacramento, and the region's cafés are getting genuinely creative with
it — think ceremonial-grade pours, flavored lattes, and tasting flights
that let you compare styles side by side. This is a running guide to the
spots worth seeking out, updated as I visit them.

*This guide is a work in progress — more spots to come.*

---

## Em Coffee House — Elk Grove

**Where:** 7440 Laguna Blvd, Suite 111, Elk Grove, CA 95758
**Phone:** (916) 897-9967 · **Web:** [emcoffeehouse.com](https://www.emcoffeehouse.com/)
**Visited:** August 2026

A bright, airy café draped in hanging greenery, Em Coffee House is a
standout for matcha lovers thanks to its **matcha flight** — the "Em
Experience" tasting set ($23 for four pours, $5.75 each). It's the best
kind of introduction to how much range matcha actually has.

The flight arrives as four small footed tasting glasses on a long wooden
paddle board, each individually labeled:

1. **Ube Matcha** — layered purple ube and green matcha over milk; the
   showstopper of the set.
2. **Matcha Latte** — a clean, classic iced matcha over milk.
3. **Matcha Einspänner** — matcha under a thick, velvety cream cap
   (Vienna/einspänner style).
4. **Matcha Coconut Blast** — topped with whipped cream and toasted
   coconut flakes for a dessert-like finish.

Every pour was good and distinct, and the flight is a genuinely fun way
to taste the spectrum in one sitting — great to share and compare. The
plant-filled, modern space makes it an easy place to linger.

> **The move:** Order the flight and work left to right, saving the
> coconut blast for last like dessert.
