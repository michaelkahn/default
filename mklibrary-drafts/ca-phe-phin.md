# Cà Phê Phin

**Where:** 7386 Stockton Blvd, Suite E, Sacramento, CA 95823
**Phone:** (916) 594-7918 · **Web:** [caphephinsac.com](https://www.caphephinsac.com/)
**Hours:** Mon–Fri 7am–6pm · Sat–Sun 7am–7pm
**Category:** Vietnamese coffee café

Cà Phê Phin is a Vietnamese coffee spot on Stockton Blvd, named for the
*phin* — the little metal drip filter that slow-brews traditional
Vietnamese coffee. But the coffee is only half the draw: the space itself
is a destination.

## The vibe

The room leans into a warm, mid-century feel and is absolutely packed
with character — lots of plants and a collection of vintage decorations
that make it feel like a lovingly curated living room. Standout pieces
include an old **television**, a **gramophone**, and a **vintage couch**,
plus plenty of other retro odds and ends throughout. It's the kind of
place you want to sit and take in for a while.

## Ordering

You've got options: order at the counter in person, or use the **self-
order kiosk / screen** if you'd rather browse and tap through yourself.

## What I tried

- **Mango matcha slushy** — a fruity, frozen twist on matcha.
- **Soursop slushy** — soursop (mãng cầu) blended into a cool, refreshing
  slush; a distinctly Vietnamese flavor.
- **Ube coffee** — coffee with ube (purple yam) for a sweet, nutty spin.

The slushies are a great call on a warm day, and the ube coffee is a fun
way to try something beyond a standard order.

## Also on the menu

- **Egg coffee** (cà phê trứng) — the rich, custardy Hanoi specialty.
- Traditional **phin-drip Vietnamese coffee** — the house's namesake pour.

## Notes

- Great pick for both the drinks and the atmosphere — worth it for the
  vintage decor alone.
