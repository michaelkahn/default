## Cà Phê Phin

Tucked into a Stockton Boulevard storefront, Cà Phê Phin (est. 2021) is a
love letter to Vietnamese coffee culture, named for the little metal
*phin* filter that slow-drips the traditional brew. The space is the
first thing that pulls you in — a warm, mid-century room absolutely packed
with character. Reclaimed wood and exposed brick, an old gramophone and a
vintage television, a Singer treadle sewing-machine table, woven palm
fans, blue-and-white porcelain, and framed black-and-white photos of old
Vietnam all come together like a lovingly curated living room, with
greenery spilling from every corner and a mix of rattan and mid-century
leather seating to sink into. You can order at the counter or tap through
the self-order kiosk.

The menu runs well beyond the classic drip. On our visit we tried the
taro coffee (cà phê môn) — a sweet, nutty, purple-tinged twist on
Vietnamese coffee — alongside a soursop slushie that was cool, tropical,
and perfect for a Sacramento summer, and a mango matcha latte layered over
oat milk. Traditionalists can still get the namesake phin-drip coffee or a
rich, custardy egg coffee (cà phê trứng). It's a spot worth visiting as
much for the atmosphere as for the caffeine.
