# Cà Phê Phin

**Where:** 7386 Stockton Blvd, Suite E, Sacramento, CA 95823
**Phone:** (916) 594-7918 · **Web:** [caphephinsac.com](https://www.caphephinsac.com/)
**Hours:** Mon–Fri 7am–6pm · Sat–Sun 7am–7pm
**Category:** Vietnamese coffee café

Cà Phê Phin is a Vietnamese coffee spot on Stockton Blvd, named for the
*phin* — the little metal drip filter that slow-brews traditional
Vietnamese coffee. But the coffee is only half the draw: the space itself
is a destination.

## The vibe

The room (the shop's been around since 2021) leans into a warm,
mid-century feel and is absolutely packed with character — it feels like
a lovingly curated living room. Reclaimed-wood and exposed-brick walls, a
corrugated-metal awning over the counter, and woven pendant lamps set the
tone, with plants spilling from every shelf and corner. The vintage
touches are the real charm: an old **gramophone** and **television**, a
**Singer treadle sewing-machine table**, blue-and-white porcelain,
hanging woven palm fans, framed black-and-white photos of old Vietnam,
and a mix of **rattan and mid-century leather seating**. It's the kind of
place you want to sit and take in for a while.

## Ordering

You've got options: order at the counter in person, or use the **self-
order kiosk** if you'd rather browse and tap through yourself. Drinks
come in the shop's own "Cà Phê Phin" cups.

## What I tried

- **Taro Coffee (Cà Phê Môn)** — iced coffee with taro; ordered regular
  sweet, less ice, oat milk. A sweet, nutty, purple-tinged spin on
  Vietnamese coffee.
- **Soursop Slushie** — soursop (mãng cầu) blended into a cool, refreshing
  frozen slush; a distinctly Vietnamese flavor and a great warm-day pick.
- **Mango Matcha Latte** — layered mango and matcha over oat milk;
  regular sweet, less ice. Fruity and bright.

A fun mix that shows the menu's range — from a traditional-leaning taro
coffee to the tropical soursop slush and the mango-matcha mashup.

## Also on the menu

- **Egg coffee** (cà phê trứng) — the rich, custardy Hanoi specialty.
- Traditional **phin-drip Vietnamese coffee** — the house's namesake pour.

## Notes

- Great pick for both the drinks and the atmosphere — worth it for the
  vintage decor alone.
